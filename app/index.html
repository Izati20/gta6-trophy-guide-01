The road to the platinum — one playthrough, four stages

There are no difficulty settings, no New Game+ and no mission replay, and free roam carries on after the credits — so the platinum is a marathon of coverage, not of skill. Launch-day guides put it at roughly 200 hours (ESTIMATE) with the bulk in stage 3. Using cheats disables trophies and saving, so keep them off a trophy run. Keep a rotating set of manual saves at the start and end of every chapter: with no mission replay, saves are your only way back.

The one warning that matters: Positive Mental Attitude

Only one trophy is even possibly missable. In Chapter 1, the morning after the mission Down to Earth, follow Lucia from the kitchen to the balcony and accept her meditation. That moment disappears after Chapter 1. Whether the trophy truly needs that spot was unconfirmed at launch — PowerPyx flagged it as a possible missable only — but every reported platinum run did it, and it costs two minutes. Everything else on this list can be earned after the story. (The 100% checklist only asks for 15 of the 18 meditation spots, so this cannot block A Long Road either way.)

Stage 1

Play the story, build the habits · Chapters 1–5

Play all five chapters and the named missions inside them — 13 story trophies land on their own, ending with the gold You Gotta Know When to Get Off. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation before Chapter 1 closes. From the first hour, make three habits automatic: scan new vehicles and WAiNK spots as you pass them, study animals through binoculars, and turn on progress and collectible notifications in Settings. Start Cal's fishing line when he texts you in Chapter 1.

Stage 2

The duo opens up · side lines and one-off moments

From Coffee Break in Chapter 2 both characters can travel together, which unlocks the partner trophies (Hot Together, Retail Therapy, Personal Best) and the last meditation spot. Work the named character lines as they appear — Kenton, Billy, Andy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret — plus the set-piece one-offs (the VCIA jet, the Mile Zero tank, the silent jewelry job). Optional missions are only offered for a while, so take them when they show up rather than banking them all for later.

Stage 3

The 100% checklist · the long gold, A Long Road

After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and grind it in geography order, not list order. In full it wants: all robberies including 8 bank vaults and 39 gang operations, underwater discoveries, all 42 Shooting Range golds, all drone races and Bret's 15 Target Decks. In part: 100 optional missions, 200 of 277 vehicle scans, 100 of 255 animal studies, 15 of 18 meditations, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts and 100 WAiNK scans. Most silver and counting trophies pop on this road. Checklist shares are as GTABase reported them on launch day.

Stage 4

Clean-up, then the platinum

Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands (they are not part of 100%), sample any activity types still blank for Master of None, clear the map fog for The Goodtime State, top up the money counters (Treat Yo Self, Dime on the Dollar) and pick off anything left — including the joke trophies whose exact triggers were still unconfirmed at launch (Bad Luck, I Guess; The Truth Is Out There; Ten Pin). When the 54th trophy lands, Vice Got a Price! closes the list on its own.

Story, free roam or online?

Story: 13 mission and chapter trophies, unmissable, plus 5 character-line finales. Free roam: everything else — robberies, races, ranges, stunts, collectibles and counters, all still available after the credits. Online: nothing. The list holds zero online trophies, so no subscription, no partners and no boosting are ever required.
